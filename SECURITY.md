# Security

Folaio runs entirely on your own computer and only listens to your own browser
(`127.0.0.1`), but we still take security seriously.

## Reporting a problem

**Please don't report security problems in public issues.** Instead, either:

- use GitHub's **private vulnerability reporting**: the *Security* tab → **Report a vulnerability**, or
- email **[talk@oprone.com](mailto:talk@oprone.com)** with "Security" in the subject.

Please include what you found, how to reproduce it, and which version you used.
We'll reply as soon as we can, keep you updated while we fix it, and credit you
(if you'd like) once a fix is released.

## Good to know

- Folaio never uploads your documents; the only internet use is downloading an
  optional Folaio Plus brain when you choose to.
- Subject Packs (`.folaio` files) from other people are treated as untrusted:
  their contents are checked and PDFs are verified before anything is added.
